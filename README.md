# Privacy Policy for Luna's Apothecary

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Luna's Apothecary respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Luna's Apothecary does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not use:
- User accounts
- Advertising trackers
- Third-party analytics
- Location tracking
- Contact collection
- Social media integrations

## Game Progress

The app may save game progress locally on your device, such as coins, inventory, planted seeds, brewed potions, completed customer orders, and settings.

This data stays on your device and is used only to make the game function correctly. It is not sent to the developer or to an external server.

## Notifications

Luna's Apothecary may ask for permission to send local notifications, for example to remind you about in-game events.

Notifications are created on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## In-App Purchases

Luna's Apothecary may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details.

Apple may process purchase-related information according to Apple's own privacy practices.

## Children's Privacy

Luna's Apothecary does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data, no personal information is knowingly stored or shared.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted in this repository.
